When enabled, all background tasks (and manual clicks) will simulate their actions without deleting files, moving torrents, or modifying your *arrs.
Unmonitor Behavior
Episodes/Albums/Books/Movies: Unmonitor individual episodes/albums/books/movies with files.
+ Complete Seasons: Also unmonitor entire seasons (Sonarr) or albums (Lidarr) when all their items are present.
+ Ended Series/Deceased Artists: Also unmonitor whole series when ended and complete, or whole artists/authors when deceased and complete.
Radarr/Whisparr always use Items Only (no parent concept).
When calculating series completeness, skip Season 0 (specials).
Before evaluating, trigger a full library refresh on each enabled *arr and wait for it to complete. Prevents unmonitoring items whose files were moved externally (e.g., failed transcodes, manual cleanup) but the *arr hasn't noticed yet. Refreshes run sequentially (not parallel) to avoid disk I/O contention. Adds 2–5 minutes on typical setups with fast *arrs only, but 15–25 minutes on large libraries where Sonarr and Lidarr walk the filesystem. If a refresh fails or times out, that *arr is skipped this cycle — no unmonitoring happens on possibly-stale data.
How long to wait for each *arr refresh before skipping that *arr this cycle. 600 works for Radarr/Whisparr/Chaptarr; Sonarr and Lidarr filesystem walks often need 1800.
